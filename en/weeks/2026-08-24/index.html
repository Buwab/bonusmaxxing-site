Method

  1. Prepare the fish burgers according to the instructions on the package.
  2. Boil the potatoes until tender, let cool and cut into cubes.

Cajun Bean Dish veggie

A spicy and nutritious bean dish with a Cajun twist. Ideal for a quick vegetarian meal.

  • 2 stuks paprika

  • 1 stuks ui
  • 2 tenen knoflook

Pantry

  • 300 g rice
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic

Also pick up

  • 1 bunch fresh coriander

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the instructions on the package.
  2. Chop the onion and bell pepper and fry in a pan with a little oil.
  3. Add the bean mix to the pan and heat briefly.
  4. Mix the rice with the vegetables and beans in the pan.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and finely chopped coriander.
  6. Serve warm with extra coriander as a garnish.

Tip. Use a little lime juice over the dish for extra freshness.
