Vegetarian Macaroni with Kidney Beans, Bell Pepper and Cheese

A midweek bean macaroni with tomato sauce, bell pepper, onion, and a layer of cheese. The kidney beans take the place of minced meat, making the dish hearty yet vegetarian.

Pantry

  • 500 ml tomato sauce
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • to taste salt
  • to taste pepper

Method

WhatsAppEmailFacebook
  1. Cook the pasta in plenty of salted water for 1 minute less than indicated on the package. Reserve a mug of cooking water and drain.
  2. Rinse the kidney beans and let them drain. Chop the onion and finely chop the garlic.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan. Sauté the onion and bell pepper for 4 to 5 minutes until they soften.
  4. Add the garlic, paprika powder, and oregano and cook for 1 minute more.
  5. Stir in the tomato sauce and kidney beans. Let simmer gently for 8 minutes, allowing the beans to absorb the sauce.
  6. Stir the pasta into the sauce. Add a splash of cooking water if the mixture becomes too thick.
  7. Stir half of the cheese into the macaroni and let it melt over low heat.
  8. Sprinkle the remaining cheese on top, cover the pan, and let it sit for another 2 minutes until the cheese softens. Season with pepper and possibly salt.

Tip. Mash a few kidney beans against the side of the pan; this will naturally thicken and enrich the tomato sauce.
