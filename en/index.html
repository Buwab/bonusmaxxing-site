Fusilli with Roasted Pepper, Chicken and Pesto

Roasted red pepper gives this pasta a sweet, Italian base. Chicken, courgette and pesto make it a quick, hearty meal.

Veggie swap Replace the chicken breast with Bonduelle Cannellini beans and warm the drained beans in the last 2 minutes.

  • 400 gr pasta bonus
  • 1 stuk courgette

  • 75 gr Parmezaanse kaas

Pantry

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
  • black pepper to taste

Method

WhatsAppEmailFacebook
  1. Preheat the oven grill. Halve the peppers, remove the seeds and place them cut side up on a baking tray lined with baking paper.
  2. Grill the peppers for 8-10 minutes, until the skin starts to blacken. Loosely cover them with a plate and let them sit for 10 minutes; then peel off most of the skin and slice the flesh into strips.
  3. Cook the fusilli according to the instructions until al dente. Reserve 150 ml of the cooking water before draining.
  4. Meanwhile, slice the chicken breast into strips and the courgette into half-moons. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan and cook the chicken for 4-5 minutes until cooked through and golden brown.
  5. Add the chopped onion, garlic, courgette and Italian herbs. Cook for 5 minutes until the courgette is just tender.
  6. Stir in the roasted pepper, pesto and 100 ml of the cooking water. Let it simmer for 2 minutes until a glossy sauce forms.
  7. Mix the fusilli into the sauce and add a splash of extra cooking water if needed. Season with black pepper and sprinkle the Parmesan cheese on top.

Veggie swap. Replace the chicken breast with Bonduelle Cannellini beans and warm the drained beans in the last 2 minutes.

Tip. Roast the peppers while the pasta water is heating; this keeps the dish well within half an hour to the table.

Chicken Coconut Curry with Pepper, Pineapple and Rice

A warm, mild curry with tender chicken, red pepper and sweet pineapple. Coconut milk makes the sauce creamy and nicely balanced with the curry.

Method

  1. Cook the brown rice according to the package instructions. Drain and keep covered with the lid.
  2. Cut the chicken breast into cubes. Slice the peppers into strips, the carrots into thin half-moons and finely chop the onion.
  3. Heat the oil in a large frying pan and cook the chicken for 4 minutes until lightly browned all over. Transfer the chicken to a plate.
  4. In the same pan, sauté the onion, carrot and pepper for 4 minutes. Add the curry powder and tomato paste and cook for 1 minute to release the aromas.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk and add the pineapple chunks along with 3 tablespoons of the pineapple juice. Bring to a boil.
  6. Return the chicken to the pan and let the curry simmer uncovered for 8-10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened slightly.
  7. Season with black pepper. Divide the rice among four bowls and spoon the chicken coconut curry over the top.

Veggie swap. Replace the chicken breast with Bonduelle Cannellini beans and add them along with the pineapple to the curry.

Tip. Use the juice from the pineapple can sparingly: a few tablespoons add freshness without making the curry too sweet.

Stuffed Peppers with Creamy Lemon Rice and Spinach veggie

Soft, oven-baked peppers are filled with rice, cannellini beans and spinach. A creamy lemon-herb topping and cheese make the dish fresh and savoury.

  • 4 stuks paprika bonus
  • 200 gr spinazie bonus
  • 480 gr cannellini bonen bonus
  • 200 ml kookroom
  • 1 stuk citroen
  • 100 gr geraspte kaas

Pantry

  • 300 g brown rice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
  • black pepper to taste

Method

WhatsAppEmailFacebook
  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Cook the brown rice according to the package instructions and drain.
  2. Halve the peppers lengthwise and remove the seeds. Place the halves cut side up in a greased baking dish.
  3. Pre-bake the pepper halves in the oven for 10 minutes so they cook through without the rice filling drying out.
  4. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in a frying pan. Sauté the chopped onion and garlic for 3 minutes, add the Italian herbs and let the spinach wilt a handful at a time.
  5. Stir in the drained Bonduelle Cannellini beans and the cooked rice into the spinach. Mix in 100 ml of cooking cream, the zest of half a lemon and black pepper.
  6. Fill the pre-baked pepper halves with the rice mixture. Mix the remaining cooking cream with 1 tablespoon of lemon juice and distribute it over the stuffed peppers.
  7. Sprinkle with grated cheese and bake for 15-18 minutes until the peppers are soft and the cheese is golden brown.

Tip. Is the lemon large? Start with 1 tablespoon of juice in the cream; you can always squeeze some extra lemon over it at the table.

Crispy Quesadillas with Cannellini Beans, Spinach and Pepper veggie

Spicy pepper, beans and spinach form a creamy filling between golden brown tortillas. A quick tomato-chilli sauce gives the quesadillas a fresh red sauce.

€7,25€2,58 saved± 30 min4/5 Bonus (80%)Healthy

4 people€7,25 at AH · €2,58 saved

+ah

For 4 people

  • 2 stuks paprika bonus
  • 200 gr spinazie bonus
  • 6 stuks tomaten wraps bonus
  • 480 gr cannellini bonen bonus
  • 150 gr geraspte kaas

Pantry

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 250 ml tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • black pepper to taste

Method

WhatsAppEmailFacebook
  1. Dice the peppers and finely chop the onion and garlic. Rinse the Bonduelle Cannellini beans and let them drain well.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan. Sauté the onion and pepper for 4 minutes over medium heat.
  3. Add the garlic, chilli powder and cumin and cook for 30 seconds. Stir in the Bonduelle Cannellini beans and lightly mash about a third of them with a spatula.
  4. Add the spinach in batches and let it wilt. Mix in 100 ml of tomato sauce and let the filling reduce for 2 minutes. Season with black pepper.
  5. Heat a dry frying pan over medium heat. On four tortillas, divide a quarter of the cheese and a quarter of the vegetable-bean filling; place the remaining tortillas on top.
  6. Cook the quesadillas, two at a time if necessary, for 2-3 minutes on each side until the tortillas are golden brown and the cheese has melted.
  7. Warm the remaining tomato sauce briefly. Cut the quesadillas into wedges and serve the warm red sauce alongside.

Tip. Let the filling reduce briefly first; this keeps the quesadillas crispy and prevents the tortillas from tearing too quickly.

Pasta e Fagioli with Broccoli and Crispy Eggs veggie

Whole wheat fusilli is mixed with creamy cannellini beans, broccoli, garlic and olive oil. A fried egg with crispy edges makes this simple pasta dish extra special.

For 4 people

  • 400 gr broccoli bonus
  • 480 gr cannellini bonen bonus
  • 400 gr pasta bonus
  • 4 stuks eieren
  • naar smaak chilivlokken

Pantry

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • black pepper to taste

Method

WhatsAppEmailFacebook
  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the fusilli according to the package instructions until al dente and add the broccoli florets for the last 4 minutes.
  2. Reserve 150 ml of the cooking water and then drain the pasta with the broccoli.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil in a large frying pan. Sauté the chopped onion for 3 minutes until soft, then add two minced cloves of garlic, oregano and optional chilli flakes.
  4. Stir in the drained Bonduelle Cannellini beans and warm through for 3 minutes. Add 100 ml of cooking water and lightly mash a small portion of the beans for a light, creamy binding.
  5. Mix the fusilli and broccoli into the beans. Add more cooking water if needed and season with black pepper.
  6. Meanwhile, heat the remaining olive oil in a small frying pan. Fry the eggs over medium heat until the whites are set and the edges are lightly crispy.
  7. Divide the pasta e fagioli among four deep plates. Place a fried egg on each plate and sprinkle the last finely chopped clove of garlic over the top.

Tip. Do not let the bean sauce dry out: a few tablespoons of pasta cooking water ensure that the fusilli and beans stick together nicely.
